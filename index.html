About me

Design with purpose.

A design-focused freelance journey built around quality, communication and client-focused creative solutions.

I’m a freelance graphic designer specializing in digital and print-focused design. I work with clients to turn ideas, references and requirements into polished visual outcomes.

My work includes vector tracing, social media graphics, print design, line art, illustration and pattern design. I also provide scalable vector artwork suitable for professional printing and large-format applications.

I started my freelance journey on Fiverr in 2024 and continue building my creative career through international client work.
